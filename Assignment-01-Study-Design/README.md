# Assignment 01 — Research Methodology & Question Formulation

## Overview

This assignment provided practical training in foundational research methodology for clinical and neuroimaging studies. The work involved applying study design principles to real-world research scenarios, evaluating systematic review literature, and developing a structured research question for a neuroimaging-based investigation.

The assignment also introduced the process of translating a research question into searchable concepts using the PICO/PECO framework and controlled vocabulary.

## Skills Developed

* Identification and interpretation of common clinical research designs
* Critical examination of systematic reviews and meta-analyses
* Formulation and structuring of research questions
* Application of PICO/PECO frameworks
* Identification of key concepts for literature searches
* Use of PubMed and MeSH terminology
* Development of search concepts using synonyms and related terms
* Basic methodological understanding of clinical and neuroimaging research

## Research Context

The practical work included examples from neurological and psychiatric research, with particular emphasis on neuroimaging, PTSD, inhibitory control, and clinical research methodology.

## Tools & Resources

PubMed, MeSH, PICO/PECO, systematic review literature
