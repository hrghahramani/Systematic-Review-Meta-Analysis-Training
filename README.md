# Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis Training

## Overview

This repository documents my practical training in systematic review methodology and evidence synthesis in neuroscience and clinical research.

The project focuses on developing practical skills in study design, research question development, literature searching, reference management, screening, eligibility assessment, and systematic review workflows.

## Training Objectives

* Understand major clinical and research study designs
* Develop research questions using PICO/PECO frameworks
* Develop systematic literature search strategies
* Use controlled vocabulary and research databases
* Manage references and identify duplicate records
* Apply predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria
* Conduct title/abstract and full-text screening
* Practice systematic review workflows and PRISMA documentation

## Tools & Databases

* PubMed
* Scopus
* Web of Science
* Embase
* PsycINFO
* EndNote
* Rayyan
* MeSH
* Emtree

## Training Assignments

### Assignment 01 — Study Design & Research Question Development

Training in identifying major clinical study designs and developing research questions using PICO/PECO frameworks.

### Assignment 02 — Systematic Literature Search & Reference Management

Practical training in developing database search strategies using Boolean operators, controlled vocabulary, MeSH, Emtree, synonyms, and related keywords, followed by reference management using EndNote.

### Assignment 03 — Screening & Systematic Review Workflow

Practical training in importing references into Rayyan, applying eligibility criteria, conducting title/abstract and full-text screening, and documenting inclusion/exclusion decisions.

### Upcoming Assignments

Additional assignments will be added as the training progresses.
