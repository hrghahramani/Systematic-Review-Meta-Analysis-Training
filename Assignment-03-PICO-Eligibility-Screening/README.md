# Assignment 03 — PICO, Eligibility & Screening

## Overview

This assignment provided practical training in translating a clinical neuroscience research question into a structured evidence-synthesis framework. The work focused on defining the research scope, establishing eligibility criteria, and applying a structured reference management and screening workflow.

## Skills Developed

* Structuring clinical research questions using PICO/PECO
* Identifying key concepts and research variables
* Developing explicit inclusion and exclusion criteria
* Defining population, intervention/exposure, comparison, and outcome components
* Managing bibliographic records using EndNote
* Identifying and resolving duplicate records
* Preparing cleaned references for systematic screening
* Conducting title/abstract and full-text screening using Rayyan
* Applying predefined eligibility criteria consistently
* Documenting study selection and screening outcomes using PRISMA

## Research Context

The practical workflow was applied to a clinical neuroscience question investigating the potential effects of bumetanide, an NKCC1 inhibitor, in patients with epilepsy, with outcomes including seizure-related measures, NKCC1/KCC2 expression, EEG findings, and treatment response.

## Tools & Frameworks

EndNote, Rayyan, PICO/PECO, PRISMA
