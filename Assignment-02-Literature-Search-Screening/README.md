Assignment 02 — Systematic Literature Search & Screening Workflow
Overview

This assignment provided hands-on training in developing and implementing a systematic literature search and screening workflow for neuroscience research. The work progressed from translating a research question into searchable concepts to managing, screening, and documenting the retrieved literature.

Skills Developed
Translating a research question into structured search concepts
Developing search strategies using Boolean operators, synonyms, and controlled vocabulary
Working with MeSH and Emtree terminology
Adapting search strategies across multiple academic databases
Conducting literature searches in PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, and PsycINFO
Managing and organizing references in EndNote
Identifying and removing duplicate records
Identifying and completing missing bibliographic information and abstracts
Preparing and exporting a final reference library
Conducting title/abstract screening and full-text assessment using predefined eligibility criteria
Applying inclusion, exclusion, and uncertainty decisions during screening
Using Rayyan to organize and document the systematic screening workflow
Research Context

The workflow was applied to a neuroscience research question investigating the role of epigenetic mechanisms in brain regions associated with relapse vulnerability in individuals with substance use disorders.

Tools & Databases

PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, PsycINFO, EndNote, Rayyan, MeSH, Emtree
